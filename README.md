# Operational Analysis & Service Efficiency of a Notary Office (SQL)

SQL analysis for measuring service duration, identifying process bottlenecks, and discerning consultation patterns within a notary office management system (undergraduate thesis in Information Systems).

> **Data note: SIMULATION.** All data in this folder is fictitious, generated by `generate_data.py` (using a random seed of 42) based on the system's database schema. It contains no real client or notary office data. The observed patterns are influenced by the parameters set within the generator; therefore, the results serve to demonstrate **analytical techniques** rather than findings from an actual notary office.

## Business questions

1. How long does each type of service take to complete, and which stage creates the most significant bottleneck?
2. Which active applications are stalled and require follow-up?
3. When are consultation periods busiest, and how frequently do scheduling conflicts occur?
4. Is the workload for document verification distributed evenly among staff members?

## Data

| Table | Content | Row |
|---|---|---|
| `users` | clients, staff, notaries | 123 |
| `katalog_layanans` | list of services | 24 |
| `jadwal_konsultasis` | consultation schedule | 420 |
| `pengajuans` | service ticket | 320 |
| `pengajuan_trackings` | time for each stage following (6 stages) | 1.889 |
| `berkas_kliens` | client documents and their verification | 1.273 |

Data period: January to September 30, 2026.

## SQL Techniques

CTEs, window functions (`LAG`, `ROW_NUMBER`, `RANK`, `SUM() OVER`), self-joins, aggregation with `CASE`, and date functions (`DATEDIFF`, `TIMESTAMPDIFF`).

## Findings (from simulation data)

| # | Findings | Figures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Longest service: **Establishment of a foreign-invested limited liability company (PT PMA)**. Fastest: **Establishment of a limited partnership (CV)** | 25.4 days vs. 11.9 days |
| 2 | The most time-consuming stage is **waiting for documents to be issued following registration with the Ministry of Law and Human Rights** | 7.3 days, 43.8% of the total time |
| 3 | For 7 active applications, there has been no progress for over 7 days, mostly during the Ministry of Law and Human Rights registration stage | 10 to 19 days of inactivity |
| 4 | Consultations peak on **Tuesdays and Thursdays** and at **10:00 AM and 2:00 PM** | Tuesday 24.0%, Thursday 22.9%; 2:00 PM: 24.0%, 10:00 AM: 22.4% |
| 5 | Schedules that overlap with other scheduled tasks (for a single notary) | 129 out of 352 active schedules (36.6%) |
| 6 | Uneven verification workload: Staff member 1 handles twice the workload of Staff member 2, who is slower and requests revisions more frequently | 67.2% vs. 32.8%; 13.4 vs. 28.0 hours; revision rates 10.0% vs. 17.3% |

Reading notes:
- Service duration is calculated only for submissions with a **Completed** status. Ongoing submissions are excluded to avoid bias in the average.
- The figure for each stage (Finding 2) represents the time elapsed from the previous stage until that stage is reached.
- Finding 5 assumes that one notary handles one client at a time. The high figure here is partly due to the fact that the simulation schedule was created without slot controls.

## Recommendations

- **Automated reminders for clients** regarding submissions that have remained stagnant for more than 7 days; also, periodically monitor the registration status with the Ministry of Law and Human Rights.
- **Set a quota for consultation slots** per hour, and limit the number of consultations on Tuesday and Thursday afternoons to prevent scheduling conflicts.
- **Distribute the verification workload** evenly among staff, and investigate the reasons for any discrepancies in processing time or revision rates between team members (e.g., differences in the use of checklists).

## How to run (MySQL 8.0+)

```sql
CREATE DATABASE notaris_analisis;
USE notaris_analisis;
SOURCE schema.sql;
SOURCE seed_data.sql;
SOURCE analisis.sql;
```

To recreate the data: `python generate_data.py`.

The query was verified by executing the entire contents of `analisis.sql` on this data. The complete results can be found in `hasil_query.txt`.

## Limitations

- The data is simulated; therefore, the figures cannot be generalized to actual notary offices.
- No statistical tests were conducted; the analysis is purely descriptive.
- There is no data regarding costs or service fees.
